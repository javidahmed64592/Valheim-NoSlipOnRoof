# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Players no longer slide off steep roofs. Vanilla slides you on anything steeper than 38 degrees, so 45 and 67 degree roofs now hold you like 26 degree ones.
- Standing still on a roof holds you in place, even on the steepest 67 degree pieces: your friction is raised while you stand still on a roof, and walking feels the same as vanilla.
